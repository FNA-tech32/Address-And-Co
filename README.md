# Address&Co

Address&Co helps buyers and sellers market, sell, and purchase property, and delivers construction, design, and supply work from Multan. The site introduces the firm, its services, its leadership, and a way to request a free quote.

## Pages

- **Home** (`index.html`) — company introduction, the services on offer, and a link to request a free analysis.
- **About** (`about.html`) — civil engineering focus, interior and exterior design, plan estimating, and project planning.
- **Service** (`service.html`) — general order supply, interior and exterior design, real estate consultancy (DHA Multan, Royal Orchard, and Citi Housing), construction and development, and architecture.
- **Team** (`team.html`) — the leadership team.
- **About CEO** (`team-detail.html`) — message and qualifications of Engr. Rana Sarmad Mahmood, CEO and founder.
- **Contact** (`contact.html`) — quote form and office details.

The Home menu also links out to related group sites: United Elite Group of Companies, United Elite Engineering Services, and United Elite Smart Energy.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the pages.

The quote form on the contact page posts to `mail.php`. Serve the folder with PHP so that form can send mail. The recipient is `hello@addressandco.com`.

```bash
php -S localhost:8000
```

Then open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000).
